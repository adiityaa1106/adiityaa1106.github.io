About

Aditya Chauhan

PhD Student in Mathematical Finance  ·  IIT Kanpur

I am a doctoral researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, working under the supervision of Prof. Joydeep Dutta. My research lies at the intersection of mathematical finance, stochastic analysis, and computational methods, with a focus on volatility modelling and portfolio optimisation.

I am particularly interested in the empirical implementation of Dupire's local volatility framework, arbitrage-free surface construction, and ESG-aware portfolio design.

Research interests

Local Volatility Models Stochastic Volatility Derivatives Pricing Measure-Theoretic Probability Portfolio Optimisation Financial Econometrics Deep Learning in Finance
