About

Aditya Chauhan

PhD Student  ·  Mathematical Finance  ·  IIT Kanpur

I am a doctoral researcher at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, working under the supervision of Prof. Joydeep Dutta. My research sits at the intersection of mathematical finance, stochastic analysis, and computational optimisation, with a focus on volatility modelling and portfolio design.

I am particularly interested in Dupire's local volatility framework, arbitrage-free surface construction, and ESG-aware sparse portfolio optimisation.

Research interests

Local Volatility Stochastic Volatility Derivatives Pricing Measure Theory Portfolio Optimisation Financial Econometrics Deep Learning in Finance

Working Papers

  • WP·01

    Sparse Portfolio Optimisation with ESG Constraints: A Two-Stage Preconditioned Proximal Approach

    Aditya Chauhan & Kanchan Mittal

    Extends the ASMP framework with an ESG dimension using a two-stage preconditioned proximal algorithm with generalised Nesterov momentum.

  • WP·02

    Arbitrage-Free Implied Volatility Surfaces using Conditional Variational Autoencoders

    Aditya Chauhan & Lavanya Pareek

    Constructs arbitrage-free implied volatility surfaces using a c-VAE architecture, with application to derivatives pricing.
